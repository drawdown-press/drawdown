Why a printed card?

The tool at drawdown.press is where the math lives. The card is where the reference lives — in your shirt pocket at the press, on the wall by the printer, at the proof desk. When you don't want to pull up a screen mid-run to check whether a ΔE of 2.4 is a pass or a fail, the card just tells you. It's the pocket version of Drawdown, and it belongs where the work happens.
